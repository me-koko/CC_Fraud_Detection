Data Governance & Privacy
Principal Component Analysis

Features V1 through V28 represent PCA orthogonal transformations provided by the original researchers to safeguard cardholder identity and satisfy banking privacy frameworks.

Standard Scaled Variables

Time (seconds offset) and Amount ($ value) were preserved in raw numerical scale, transformed dynamically via standard normal scaling during preprocessing.

Undersampling Methodology

Because only 492 out of 284,807 transactions are fraudulent, a naive classifier predicting all legit would score 99.83% accuracy while detecting 0 fraud. Random undersampling of legitimate transactions was applied to achieve balanced training and maximize sensitivity.
